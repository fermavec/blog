GlucoMentor

Intelligent assistant for comprehensive diabetes management.

GlucoMentor is an innovative web application designed to transform the tracking and management of diabetes. This tool enables rigorous and accessible monitoring of fundamental metrics: glucose levels, physical activity, carbohydrate intake, and medication administration.

Leveraging the artificial intelligence capabilities of Gemini, GlucoMentor offers a hybrid interaction model that facilitates daily use. Users can input their data manually, or via voice commands. This voice registration processes natural language to classify and organize multiple metrics simultaneously, reducing friction and the time required for health tracking.

The main page features a specialized metrics dashboard for people with diabetes, visualizing health trends clearly to facilitate clinical decision-making and improve quality of life.
